## About Me

I am a **Biostatistician and Health Data Scientist** with a background in biomedical research, clinical data analysis, and statistical methodology.

My work focuses on transforming complex biomedical and clinical data into meaningful scientific insights. I am particularly interested in **biomarker discovery, longitudinal data analysis, predictive modeling, and statistical learning**, with applications in clinical research and translational medicine.

I primarily work with **R and Python**, combining statistical rigor with computational approaches to address challenging analytical problems.

My areas of expertise and interest include:

- **Biostatistics:** Univariate and multivariate analysis, hypothesis testing, survival analysis, longitudinal data analysis, and mixed-effects models (LMM / GLMM).
- **Machine Learning:** Predictive modeling, feature selection, anomaly detection, imbalanced classification, and neural networks.
- **Scientific Computing:** Data preprocessing, exploratory data analysis, statistical programming, reproducible workflows, and visualization.
- **Data Engineering:** Data pipelines, data validation, workflow automation, SQL, Docker, and reproducible environments.
- **Software Development:** Interactive applications, scientific tools, and web-based data visualization.

My approach is built around three principles: **scientific rigor, reproducibility, and engineering quality.**
