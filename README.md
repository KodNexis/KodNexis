## About Me

I am a **Biostatistician and Health Data Scientist** with a background in biomedical research, clinical data analysis, and statistical methodology.

My work focuses on transforming complex biomedical and clinical datasets into meaningful scientific insights. I am particularly interested in **biomarker discovery, longitudinal data analysis, predictive modeling, and statistical learning**, with applications in clinical research and translational medicine.

I primarily work with **R and Python**, combining statistical rigor with computational approaches to solve challenging analytical problems.

My technical interests extend beyond statistical modeling to include **Data Engineering, software development, and reproducible computational environments**.

I believe that high-quality data science requires three essential foundations:

- **Scientific rigor:** Sound statistical methodology and reliable analytical results.
- **Reproducibility:** Transparent, documented, and automated workflows.
- **Engineering quality:** Robust data pipelines and maintainable software solutions.
